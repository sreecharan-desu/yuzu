Yuzu field notes

Yuzu is a citrus fruit with an intensely fragrant rind. See the reference.

Where it grows

  • Japan, especially Kochi
  • Korea, where it is called yuja
  • China, its likely origin

Uses

  1. Ponzu sauce
  2. Yuzu kosho
  3. Winter baths on the solstice

Measured juice yield was 18% by weight, with café-style tasting notes & a sharp finish.
