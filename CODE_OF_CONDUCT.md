# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Yuzu to be a welcoming project for everyone, whatever their age, body size, visible or
invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or
sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Good behaviour includes:

- being kind and patient with others
- respecting different viewpoints and experiences
- giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them

Unacceptable behaviour includes:

- harassment, insults or personal attacks
- sexualised language or imagery
- publishing other people's private information without permission
- any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that
don't follow this code of conduct, and may ban contributors who repeatedly or seriously violate it.

Report problems privately to the maintainer through
[GitHub](https://github.com/sreecharan-desu). All reports will be handled confidentially.
